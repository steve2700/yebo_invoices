# Photos for the landing and tenders pages

The pages show nothing here until you add real photos. There are no stand-in pictures on purpose.

## How to add one
1. Put the image in this folder (JPG or WebP, around 1600px wide).
2. Add an entry to `credits.json`:

```json
[
  {
    "file": "tiler-bathroom.jpg",
    "alt": "A tiler laying floor tiles in a bathroom in Soweto",
    "credit": "Sipho Dlamini",
    "license": "own photo",
    "source": "https://example.com/where-it-came-from"
  }
]
```

`credit` and `alt` are required. The page shows the credit under the photo.

## Best source: your own jobs
Photos from Granite Carpentry and The Timber Studio jobs are the strongest option. They are real, they are yours, and no licence applies. Ask the client before using a photo that shows their home or their face.

## Free libraries, if you need more
Check the licence on each photo page and keep the credit.
- Wikimedia Commons: search "South Africa" plus a trade. Licences vary (CC BY, CC BY-SA, public domain). Credit and licence are required for most.
- Unsplash and Pexels: free to use. A credit is polite and keeps you safe if their terms change.

Do not use AI generated images of people or workshops. They are the quickest way to make the page look fake.
