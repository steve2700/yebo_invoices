# Leads and local pages (plan, not built yet)

Status: **idea, to be validated before any code.** Written 31 October 2026.

## The idea
Each business already has a request-a-quote page. For businesses on a paid leads plan, that page becomes a public, search-friendly page such as
`yeboinvoices.com/pros/door-installation/midrand/granite-carpentry`, so people searching Google for "door installation Midrand" can find the business and ask for a quote. The request lands in the same Quote requests inbox the owner already uses. Yebo then brings the owner new quotes, not just tools to manage the ones they have.

## Hard truths
- Pages do not bring leads. **Traffic does,** and a new site ranks slowly. Expect months, not weeks, for organic results.
- Local searches are crowded by directories and Google Maps listings. The Google Business Profile of each client matters as much as our pages.
- Thousands of thin, near-identical pages can be treated as spam by Google. Every indexed page needs real content.
- Leads must be real and routed to the right business. Bad leads kill trust faster than no leads.

## What makes a page worth indexing
Business description, services, the towns they cover, work photos, reviews (we already collect them), years in business, guarantee, a price guide ("from R…"), and LocalBusiness structured data. Only service and town combinations the business actually serves get a page. A page with too little content stays hidden.

## Who gets indexed
- Request pages stay **noindex** (hidden from Google) by default. This is today's behaviour.
- Only businesses on the leads plan are switched to **index** and listed in the sitemap. Cancel the plan and the page goes back to noindex.

## How it grows
1. **Prove it first.** 3 to 5 friendly businesses for 60 days, free. Measure visits, requests and real jobs.
2. **Google Ads as the experiment.** A small budget on 3 or 4 searches shows the true cost of a lead within days. It sets the price per lead, and it brings leads while organic ranking builds.
3. **Content that earns links.** Cost guides built from real (anonymised, with consent) Yebo quote data, for example "What does door installation cost in Gauteng in 2026?". Nobody else has this data.
4. **Google Business Profile setup** for each client, with the request page as the booking link.

## Domain
Keep it on **yeboinvoices.com in a folder** (`/pros/...`), not a new domain or a subdomain. A folder shares the authority the site is already earning (the press release and the pages Google has already indexed). A separate consumer brand can come later if this works. Do not split early.

## Pricing (proposal, to validate with real lead costs)
| Option | What the business gets | Price idea |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Free | Request link, QR code, source tags (hidden from Google) | R0 |
| Local page | Indexed page in the directory, Google Business Profile guidance | R199 to R299 a month |
| Pay per lead | Only pay for verified leads sent to them | R80 to R200 a lead, set from the real ad cost plus margin |
| Growth | We run Google Ads for the business | Ad budget paid by them plus a management fee (about 20 percent, minimum applies) |

A flat **R1500 a month** is too much before there is proof. Charge it only when it includes managed ads and a clear lead promise. Start with a guarantee: the first 3 leads are free, and a refund if no real lead arrives in 60 days.

## Needed before launch
- A privacy notice and consent text on the public form (POPIA).
- Phone verification of the person asking for a quote, to cut spam.
- A rule for how many businesses can receive the same lead (one is best).
- Lead refund process for fake or unreachable leads.
- A sitemap, canonical links and structured data on indexed pages.
- Admin view: leads per business and per source, cost per lead.
